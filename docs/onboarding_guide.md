## First week
New employees receive their laptop on the first day. Orientation is held every Monday at 09:30 in the main conference room. During the first week, each new employee is paired with a buddy from their team.

## Probation
The probation period lasts 3 months. A review meeting with your manager takes place at the end of month 2. Employees who pass probation become eligible for remote work and the full benefits package.

## Training
All employees must complete the data protection course within 30 days of joining. The security awareness course must be repeated every year.

## Who to ask
Questions about payroll go to the HR helpdesk. Questions about laptops and accounts go to the IT service desk.
