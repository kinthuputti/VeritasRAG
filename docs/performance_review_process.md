## Review cycle
Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December. Each review covers the previous six months of work.

## Ratings and preparation
Employees are rated on a scale of 1 to 5. The self-assessment form is due 7 days before the review meeting. Managers must share written feedback within 5 working days after the meeting.

## Performance improvement
An employee rated 1 may be placed on a performance improvement plan. A performance improvement plan lasts 90 days and includes fortnightly check-ins with the manager.
