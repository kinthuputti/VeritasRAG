---
title: Data Retention Policy
access: all
---
## Systems
System logs are retained for 90 days. Backups are taken daily and kept for 35 days.

## Customer data
Customer data is deleted within 30 days after the contract ends, unless the law requires it to be kept longer. Deletion requests from customers are confirmed in writing.

## Email
Email in shared mailboxes is archived after 2 years.
