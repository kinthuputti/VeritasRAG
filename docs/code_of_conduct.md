## Gifts and hospitality
Gifts or hospitality worth more than 50 dollars from a client or supplier must be declared to your manager. Cash gifts are never acceptable.

## Conflicts of interest
A conflict of interest must be declared in writing within 5 working days of becoming aware of it. Examples include a family member working for a supplier.

## Speaking up
Concerns about misconduct can be reported through the confidential whistleblowing line. Every report is acknowledged within 24 hours, and reports may be made anonymously.

## Use of company name
Employees may not use the company name in political campaigns or personal endorsements.
