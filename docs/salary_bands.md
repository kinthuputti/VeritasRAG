## Salary bands
Band L3 (junior engineer) ranges from 48,000 to 62,000 dollars per year. Band L4 (engineer) ranges from 62,000 to 85,000 dollars. Band L5 (senior engineer) ranges from 85,000 to 112,000 dollars.

## Annual bonus
The annual bonus target is 8 percent of base salary for bands L3 and L4, and 12 percent for band L5. Bonuses are paid in March. Employees on a performance improvement plan are not eligible for a bonus.

## Salary reviews
Salary reviews take place every April. The standard merit increase budget is 4 percent. A promotion increase is capped at 10 percent in a single cycle.
