## Passwords and access
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and changed every 180 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company accounts. Passwords must never be shared, even with IT staff.

## Devices
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption. Personal devices may access company email only through the approved mobile app. USB storage devices are blocked by default.

## Reporting incidents
Report a lost device or a suspected phishing email to the security team within 1 hour, using the address security@calderfinch.example. Do not delete a suspected phishing email before reporting it.

## Software
Only software from the approved catalogue may be installed. New software requests are reviewed by IT within 5 working days.
