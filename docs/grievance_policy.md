## Raising a grievance
A grievance must be raised in writing within 60 days of the event it concerns. Grievances are submitted to the HR helpdesk or to any HR business partner.

## Response and appeal
HR responds to every grievance within 10 working days. If the employee disagrees with the outcome, they may appeal to a director within 14 days of receiving the decision. The director's decision is final.

## Protection
Nobody may be treated unfavourably for raising a grievance in good faith.
