## Annual leave
Every full-time employee receives 24 days of annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave accrues at 2 days per month. Up to 8 unused days may be carried over to the next year, and carried-over days expire on 31 March.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required when sick leave lasts more than 3 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried over.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave. Parental leave must begin within 6 months of the birth or adoption.

## How to request leave
Submit leave requests through the HR portal at least 5 working days before the start date. Your manager approves annual leave. Unplanned sick leave must be reported to your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.
