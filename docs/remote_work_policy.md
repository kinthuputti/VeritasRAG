## Eligibility
Employees may work remotely after completing their 3-month probation period. Employees on probation work from the office.

## Remote days
Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Every team must be in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 15:00 local time.

## Equipment and allowance
The company provides one laptop and one monitor per employee. Remote employees receive a home-office allowance of 600 dollars, paid once. Internet costs are not reimbursed.

## Working from another country
Working from another country for more than 14 days in a year needs written approval from HR, because of tax and legal rules.
