## Submitting expenses
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date. Claims submitted after 30 days will not be reimbursed. Every claim needs an itemised receipt. Card slips alone are not accepted.

## Meals and entertainment
The daily meal allowance during business travel is 45 dollars. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Client entertainment above 150 dollars per event needs written approval from a director.

## Approval limits
Managers can approve expenses up to 500 dollars. Directors can approve expenses up to 5,000 dollars. Any expense above 5,000 dollars requires approval from the Chief Financial Officer.

## Reimbursement timing
Approved expenses are paid on the 15th of the following month, directly into the employee's salary account.
