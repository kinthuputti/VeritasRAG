## Opening hours
The office opens at 07:30 and closes at 20:00 on working days. Out-of-hours access needs approval from facilities.

## Visitors
All visitors must register at reception and wear a visitor badge at all times. Visitors must be accompanied by an employee.

## Meeting rooms
Meeting rooms can be booked up to 14 days ahead through the room booking calendar. Bookings unclaimed after 15 minutes are released.

## Lockers
Lockers are available on a first-come basis and must be emptied every Friday evening.
