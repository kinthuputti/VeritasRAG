## Month-end close
The month-end close is completed by the 5th working day of the following month. Accruals must be posted by 18:00 on the 2nd working day. Intercompany balances are reconciled on the 3rd working day.

## Quarter-end
Quarter-end close takes 8 working days. The audit committee receives the draft quarterly report on the 10th working day.

## Controls
Journal entries above 25,000 dollars need a second approver. Bank reconciliations must be signed off by the Finance Controller within 2 working days of the statement date.
