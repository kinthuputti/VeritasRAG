## Quotes and approved suppliers
Purchases above 2,000 dollars need three written quotes. Only suppliers approved by the procurement team may be used for recurring purchases.

## Purchase orders
Purchase orders above 10,000 dollars need sign-off from a director before they are sent. Orders may not be split to avoid an approval limit.

## Payment
The standard payment term for suppliers is 30 days from the invoice date. Urgent payments must be requested through the procurement desk.
