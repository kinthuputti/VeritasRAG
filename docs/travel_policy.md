## Booking
All flights and hotels must be booked through the company travel desk. Flights under 6 hours are booked in economy class. Flights of 6 hours or longer may be booked in premium economy.

## Hotels
The hotel limit is 180 dollars per night in major cities and 120 dollars per night elsewhere. Book at least 14 days ahead where possible.

## Advance approval
Every business trip needs manager approval before booking. International trips also need approval from a director. Trips cancelled less than 48 hours before departure must be reported to the travel desk immediately.

## Per diem
Staff on trips receive a per diem of 30 dollars for incidental costs, paid for each full day of travel.
